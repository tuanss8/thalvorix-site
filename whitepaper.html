1. Summary

Thalvorix is an NFT game on BNB Chain. Players collect Hero NFTs (max 6,666), battle in Duel Arena Battle Card, and win Mystery Boxes containing items.

THX is the Thalvorix utility token. A total of 1,000,000,000 THX was minted. 200,000,000 THX was permanently burned on November 11, 2026 when the first presale contract was closed, so the effective supply is 800,000,000 THX. THX is used for game rewards (Zealy, Burn Box) and will become the in-game currency.

The THX presale is live now, November 11–25, 2026, at 1 USDT = 20,000 THX (1 THX = $0.00005). Hero NFT holders get an 80% discount, with the discount quota depending on the rarity tier of the NFTs they hold.

This whitepaper explains: tokenomics, presale, burn, and liquidity.

Important note: The “THX” ticker is also used by another project (THX Network, Ethereum). Thalvorix THX is a different token, on BNB Chain, with the contract above. Always verify the contract address before buying.

  • Max. buy: 5 BNB per transaction.

  • After the discount quota is used, the holder can keep buying at the normal price — the discount cap only limits how much can be bought at the discount price, not how much can be bought in total.

5. Burn

Thalvorix uses burn to reduce supply:

  1. First presale closure burn: 200,000,000 THX burned on November 11, 2026 (the unsold allocation of the first presale contract). This is already done and verifiable on BscScan.
  2. Initial burn: 25,000,000 THX (from the tokenomics allocation) burned at listing.
  3. Presale remainder burn: all unsold presale THX is burned at finalize().
  4. Ongoing burn: additional burn mechanisms (e.g. from game fees) will be announced separately.

All burns are verifiable on BscScan (transfers to the dead address).

6. Liquidity & 1-Year Lock

After the presale ends:

  1. 60% of the BNB raised + 150,000,000 THX (liquidity allocation) is added as liquidity on PancakeSwap (THX/BNB pair).
  2. LP tokens are LOCKED for 1 year (365 days) through a third-party locker (PinkLock / Team Finance). During the lock, no one can remove the liquidity — including the team.
  3. Lock proof (PinkLock link) will be published on thalvorix.xyz and X.
  4. At the presale price (1 THX = $0.00005), the listing price is about $0.0001 (2x the presale price), with a fully diluted valuation of about $100,000.

The remaining 40% of BNB raised is used for operations, development, and marketing.
